## EASA

The full Air Operations compilation includes the regulations, certification specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material.

- Subpart ORO.FTL starts on PDF page **854**; CS FTL.1 starts on page **874**. The substantive FTL section ends partway through page **886**. Appendix I begins on that page; pages 887–888 retain the FTL running header but contain the declaration form.
- [Official publication page](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-air-operations)
- [Original PDF download](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/downloads/20342/en)

## FAA

Part 117 covers flight and duty limitations and rest requirements for flightcrew members, primarily in Part 121 passenger operations. Tables A, B and C are on PDF page **8**. The official extract also includes the end of Part 111 on its first page.

- [Official 2026 PDF from GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2026-title14-vol3/pdf/CFR-2026-title14-vol3-part117.pdf)
- [Local 2026 XML](faa-14-cfr-part-117-2026.xml): downloaded unchanged from [GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2026-title14-vol3/xml/CFR-2026-title14-vol3-part117.xml) on 2026-09-23. 44,437 bytes; parsed successfully and Part 117 heading and Tables A–C verified. Same annual edition as the local PDF.
